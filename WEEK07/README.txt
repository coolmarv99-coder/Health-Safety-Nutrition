DESIGN
- Culminates the Weeks 3–7 NOTICE → ASSESS → PREVENT → RESPOND sequence.
- Includes an evidence-lens scan separating objective observation,
  interpretation, and investigation.
- Includes a child-disclosure scenario requiring calm, non-leading support,
  exact documentation, and mandated action without promises of secrecy.
- Preserves warning signs versus proof, risk and protective factors,
  individual reporting duty, confidentiality, and trauma-sensitive support.
- Randomizes conventional answer choices while preserving explanatory feedback.
- Preserves the shared shell and existing Maya image filename/path.

CONTENT NOTE
This chapter addresses child abuse and neglect. The guide avoids graphic detail and focuses on
professional recognition, prevention, reporting, documentation, and supportive care.

Station 2

Quality in action

High-quality programs do more than keep children occupied. They intentionally connect environments, relationships, curriculum, assessment, and family partnership.

Program A

The room is clean and well supplied. Teachers use one curriculum for every child and contact families mainly when problems arise.

Program B

Teachers build warm relationships, plan achievable challenges, observe children to adjust experiences, and invite families’ goals and knowledge.

Station 4 · Signature interaction

Whole-child scenario

The Garden Room

A teacher notices that several children are restless late in the morning. The room becomes crowded during transitions. Snack is often rushed, one child has difficulty opening the food sent from home, and families receive few updates about classroom routines. Staff members are warm with children but say they have no time to review observations together.

Select every influence that deserves attention.

Whole-child move: Rather than finding one person to blame, look for connected program conditions that adults can intentionally improve.

DESIGN CONTINUITY
This guide retains the established CLDV-05 shell, responsive layout, progress indicator, immediate explanatory feedback, randomized conventional answer choices, reflection, reset controls, and weekly Maya identity.

NUTRITION TRANSITION
Week 13 explicitly introduces the Nutrition reasoning cycle:
CHOOSE → BALANCE → ADAPT → SUPPORT

“Food Is Fuel, Culture, and Comfort” uses a signature Build the Snack interaction. Students manipulate concrete food and beverage choices, evaluate variety and balance, adapt for culture and family context, and distinguish supportive feeding from pressure. Menu, beverage, nutrient, and child-refusal decisions avoid simplistic “good food/bad food” framing.

WEEK 11 INSTRUCTIONAL PERSONALITY
“The Care Plan Is a Promise” centers individualized health planning as a bridge between health protection and inclusion. Its signature care-plan lab asks students to identify when a situation calls for baseline information, routine support, emergency steps, or team communication. Additional applications address objective documentation, collaborative roles, plan completeness, independence, confidentiality, updates, and field-trip readiness.

The guide reinforces that educators implement authorized plans and document observable facts; they do not diagnose or improvise medical treatment.

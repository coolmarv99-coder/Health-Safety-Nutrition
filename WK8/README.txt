DESIGN
- Introduces OBSERVE → COMPARE → SUPPORT → ACT as the Health reasoning cycle.
- Includes a Morning Health Check with ordinary variation, monitoring,
  documentation/communication, and prompt-action decisions.
- Includes paired notes that distinguish objective observation from diagnosis.
- Reinforces patterns over isolated signs and educator-versus-clinician roles.
- Preserves screening, oral health, sleep, family partnership, health records,
  and staff-wellness content from Paris Chapter 7.
- Randomizes conventional answer choices while preserving explanatory feedback.
- Preserves the shared CLDV-05 shell and existing Maya image filename/path.

DESIGN CONTINUITY
This guide retains the established CLDV-05 visual shell, responsive layout, progress indicator, immediate explanatory feedback, answer-order randomization, reflection, reset control, and Health reasoning cycle:
OBSERVE → COMPARE → SUPPORT → ACT

WEEK 12 INSTRUCTIONAL PERSONALITY
“Feelings Count as Health Too” centers behavior as information rather than diagnosis. Its signature Pattern Studio asks students to distinguish calm first support, further observation, collaborative consultation, and urgent protective action. Additional applications address objective documentation, meaningful patterns, trauma-responsive care, family partnership, professional boundaries, and preparation for the Health Mini-Exploration.

The guide reinforces that educators build protective relationships and environments, document observable facts, and collaborate appropriately; they do not diagnose, investigate trauma, or provide therapy.

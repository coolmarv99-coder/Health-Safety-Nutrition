2 · Connect

Development changes the feeding plan

The same broad goal—good nutrition—requires different adult actions as children mature.

Infants

Individual plans, responsive bottle feeding, safe preparation, readiness for solids.

Toddlers

Small portions, routines, close supervision, and growing self-feeding skills.

Preschoolers

Repeated exposure, choices, adult modeling, and low-pressure responses to picky eating.

School age

Substantial snacks, growing energy needs, participation, and meals across settings.

WEEK 14 INSTRUCTIONAL PERSONALITY
“Protecting Wellness, Not Just Serving Food” uses a six-scenario Wellness Protection Roundtable. Students distinguish reliable food systems, routine inclusive support, participation adaptations, and partnership around meaningful patterns. Applications integrate allergy management, choking prevention, enjoyable physical activity, food access, hydration, whole-child wellness, and freedom from body or food shame.

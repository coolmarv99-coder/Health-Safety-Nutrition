Maya is helping review a weekly menu. At first, it looks like a simple list: breakfast, snack, lunch, snack. Maya looks again. She notices color, texture, portions, food groups, vegetarian options, family-style service, refrigeration, handwashing, leftovers, choking hazards, and food from home. Health, safety, and nutrition no longer seem like separate topics.
